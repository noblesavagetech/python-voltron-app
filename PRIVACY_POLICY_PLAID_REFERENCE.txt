IMPORTANT NOTICE ABOUT PLAID INTEGRATION
=========================================

BBA Services uses Plaid Inc. ("Plaid") to gather your data from financial institutions. 

By using the BBA Services platform, you grant BBA Services and Plaid the right, power, and 
authority to act on your behalf to access and transmit your personal and financial information 
from your relevant financial institution. You agree to your personal and financial information 
being transferred, stored, and processed by Plaid in accordance with Plaid's privacy policy.

When you provide login data and additional information needed to connect your accounts, you 
also give Plaid permission and authority to act on your behalf to access and transmit data to 
and from your financial institution.

PLAID DATA COLLECTION AND PROCESSING
====================================

Our mission at Plaid is to unlock financial freedom for everyone. Privacy and security are 
very important to us.

This reference document is meant to help you understand how Plaid collects, uses, and shares 
your data when you use Plaid products or services through BBA Services - for example, when 
you use Plaid to connect and share your data to power the BBA Services application.

Data Plaid Collects:

1. Data you provide:
   - Identifiers like name, email address, date of birth, Social Security number, and phone number
   - Login data when required by the provider of your account (username, password, account and 
     routing number, or security token)
   - Additional information needed to connect your accounts (security questions and answers, 
     one-time passwords)
   - Information from documents or statements (bank statements or pay stubs)
   - Information related to your use of services

2. Data from financial institutions when you connect your financial account:
   - Account data (financial institution name, account name, account type, account ownership, 
     account number, routing number)
   - Account balance data (current and available balance)
   - Credit account data (due dates, balances owed, payment amounts and dates, transaction 
     history, credit limit, repayment status, interest rate)
   - Loan account data (due dates, repayment status, balances, payment amounts and dates, 
     interest rate, loan type, payment plan, terms)
   - Investment account data (transaction information, type of asset, identifying details, 
     quantity, price, fees)
   - Account owner identifiers (name, email address, phone number, date of birth, address)
   - Transaction data (amount, date, payee, type, quantity, price, location, description)
   - Payroll and tax document data (income and employer information)

3. Data from the electronic device you use:
   - Internet protocol (IP) address
   - Timezone setting and location, device location
   - Hardware model and operating system
   - Features accessed within services
   - Browser data and network data
   - Other technical data about the device

HOW PLAID USES YOUR DATA
=========================

Plaid uses your data for the following purposes:

- Provide Services: To operate, provide, service, assess, process, and maintain products and 
  services
- Help Prevent Fraud and Verify Identity: To verify your identity and help protect against 
  fraud, unauthorized transactions, and security threats
- Develop Services: To improve, enhance, and develop new products, features, and technologies
- Provide Support: To provide support and respond to inquiries
- Communicate: To send technical notices, updates, security alerts, and messages
- For Legal Purposes: To comply with legal obligations and establish/defend against claims

HOW PLAID SHARES YOUR DATA
===========================

As permitted by law, Plaid may share your data:

- With BBA Services (the app you are using) and as directed by BBA Services
- With the financial institutions you connect to Plaid or to BBA Services
- With data processors and service providers who perform services for Plaid or BBA Services
- To comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process
- In connection with a change in ownership or control of Plaid's business
- Between Plaid and its affiliates, subsidiaries and related companies
- To protect against fraud, unauthorized transactions, claims, or other liabilities
- For any other notified purpose with your consent

Plaid does not share your data with non-affiliated third parties except as permitted by law.

PLAID DATA RETENTION
====================

Plaid retains your data only as long as it is needed. Plaid reviews your data periodically 
to ensure it is still needed. When BBA Services removes your connection, Plaid's systems are 
designed to automatically delete your personal data, subject to certain exceptions where 
retention is necessary for:

- Continuing to provide services you requested
- Legal compliance requirements
- Fraud prevention and security purposes
- Data that has been aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized

PLAID SECURITY
==============

Plaid's security policies and practices are designed to protect the confidentiality and 
integrity of your data. Plaid implements controls designed to limit access to data to 
personnel who have a business reason to know it and prohibits personnel from unlawfully 
accessing, using or disclosing this data.

YOUR DATA PROTECTION RIGHTS WITH PLAID
======================================

Regardless of where you live, you have the following rights related to your personal data 
processed by Plaid:

- Access data collected about you
- Request details about categories and specific pieces of personal information collected
- Request rectification or update of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Request erasure or restriction of processing of your data (under certain circumstances)
- Object to processing of your data under certain conditions
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Request data portability (receive data in a structured, machine-readable format)

To exercise these rights with Plaid, you can:
- Submit a request using Plaid's online form
- Contact Plaid at: privacy@plaid.com
- Use Plaid Portal at my.plaid.com (for U.S. users) to view and manage connections

PLAID PORTAL
============

Plaid developed the Plaid Portal to provide you with a convenient, centralized way to view 
and manage the connections you've made using Plaid.

If you're located in the U.S., you can sign up for Plaid Portal by visiting my.plaid.com, 
verifying your phone number and email address, and creating a password. The Plaid Portal 
provides you with controls to:

- View financial accounts you've connected to BBA Services using Plaid
- See the types of data shared with BBA Services
- Terminate the connection between BBA Services and your financial accounts
- Delete associated data stored in Plaid's systems
